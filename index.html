Problem Statement & Scope

Solving Everyday Vegetable Shopping Hurdles

Visiting physical vegetable markets (mandis) is an essential routine for millions of households. However, traditional market visits often introduce common practical hurdles:

  • ✕ Opaque pricing: Hard to know if you're getting a fair rate without haggling.
  • ✕ Manual comparison: Walking between 10+ crowded stalls just to compare basic prices.
  • ✕ Uncertain stock: Not knowing if specific fresh items are available before visiting.
  • ✕ Cost unpredictability: Difficult to tally the exact basket total prior to purchase.

Our Digital Approach

"Vegetable Market" does not seek to displace local vendors. Instead, it offers a practical, student-designed digital layer connecting local sellers with households—providing transparent pricing, inventory visibility, and convenient doorstep delivery.

Project Technical Specifications

Project Title: Vegetable Market
Project Type: College Mini-Project / Demonstration
Frontend Stack: HTML5, CSS3 (Custom Tokens), Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
State Storage: Client-side Web Storage (localStorage)
Data Representation: 22 Regional Indian Vegetables, 5 Local Sellers
Simulations: Demo Checkout, UPI QR, 4-Stage Live Order Stepper
Accessibility: Semantic HTML, ARIA Live, Keyboard Accessible
